### #1: C5 — Dice-Placement Roguelite (daily-seed-first)

**"Roll dice, place into a grid, trigger scoring combos. Daily seed = everyone faces the same rolls. Free. Fair. Daily."**

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Hook clarity | 5 | "Drop dice, chase combos, beat today's score" — 5-second explainable |
| Replayability | 5 | Combinatorial (grid positions × dice values × charms); daily seed renews |
| Retention | 4 | Daily seed ritual + leaderboard flex + charm unlock progression |
| Monetization | 4 | RV: extra reroll, continue, charm preview. Interstitial: between runs. Remove-ads SKU. |
| UA/creative | 5 | Balatro multiplier-cascade is proven thumb-stop; CTR 4–6% [SPEC]; testable pre-build |
| Technical simplicity | 4 | Turn-based, discrete grid, no physics, no realtime — Claude Code confident |
| Content lightness | 5 | Seeded/systemic; charms are stat modifiers (text + icon), no authored levels |
| LiveOps lightness | 5 | Daily seed auto-generates; weekly "gauntlet" is a parameter change |
| Originality | 4 | Dice-grid + scoring synergies + F2P daily seed = unclaimed mobile niche |
| Test suitability | 5 | Clean metrics: daily-seed completion rate, D1/D7, RV opt-in, daily return |

**Total: 84/100 — EXCEPTIONAL**

**Whitespace:** No F2P mobile-native dice-placement roguelite exists. Dice A Million (PC, premium) is incoming but not yet mobile. Balatro is premium. The free daily-seed ladder has no owner.

**Creative:** Honest CTR 4–6%. Multiplier-cascade clips are native TikTok content. Daily-seed share (emoji score grid) provides organic loop. Testable with $300 concept footage pre-build.

**Red-team fix applied:** Ship daily-seed-first, NOT full Balatro meta-progression. The seed IS the retention — it converts "thin Balatro clone" into "daily brain-duel." Charm depth grows later.

**Key risks:**
- Balatro-clone fatigue by mid-2026 (mitigate: position as "daily dice duel," not "Balatro-like")
- Charm balance needs sim harness eventually (mitigate: ship with 8–12 simple charms, expand post-launch)
- Play gambling policy (mitigate: frame rerolls as "choice," no slot/gacha aesthetic)

**International viability:** Universal. Dice/numbers/combos have no cultural barrier. Roguelite audience is global, skewing US/EU/East Asia.

### #2: C2 — Rising Tide Block Puzzle (tide-as-daily-mode)

**"Clear blocks before the tide drowns your board. Zen mode = relax. Daily Tide = the shareable survival challenge."**

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Hook clarity | 5 | "Block puzzle + rising water" — instant, visual, one screenshot |
| Replayability | 4 | Procedural piece sequence; daily seed for tide mode; zen is infinite |
| Retention | 4 | Zen mode retains the mass casual audience; daily tide adds a ritual layer |
| Monetization | 5 | RV: sandbag revive (proven willingness-to-pay in pressure games), undo, hint. Interstitial: between games. Highest impression density of the set. |
| UA/creative | 5 | Highest honest CTR in portfolio (4.5–7%); hits full hook stack (failure-bait + satisfying + near-miss). Algorithm-legible category. Testable pre-build. |
| Technical simplicity | 5 | Grid-based, no physics engine needed, block-fit is deterministic logic |
| Content lightness | 5 | Procedural piece generation; no authored content |
| LiveOps lightness | 5 | Daily seed auto-generates; no event calendar needed |
| Originality | 3 | Tide twist is genuine but the base is the most-cloned category on earth |
| Test suitability | 5 | Block puzzle metrics are the most benchmarked in mobile — clean read |

**Total: 80/100 — EXCEPTIONAL**

**Whitespace:** Block puzzle base is CROWDED (Block Blast ~870M downloads). But: no casual 8×8 title ships a rising-flood mechanic. The twist is unclaimed. Positioned as "the arcade cousin," not a replacement.

**Creative:** Best honest-creative concept in the set. The drowning/clear moment IS the ad. CTR 4.5–7% [SPEC]. Block puzzle is proven-legible to ad algorithms.

**Red-team fix applied:** Tide is quarantined to daily/challenge mode. Zen mode (no tide, no fail pressure) captures the mass relaxation audience. Tide becomes the spicy, shareable variant — not the core that fights the genre's retention engine.

**Key risks:**
- Competing on UA cost vs Block Blast's LTV-funded bids (mitigate: differentiate on creative, not keyword)
- Polish/juice gap vs 8-figure incumbents (mitigate: scope to one mode at a time; juice spike in week 1)
- "Drown = stress" alienates relax-puzzle audience (mitigate: zen mode is the default; tide is opt-in daily)

**International viability:** Maximum. Block puzzle is the most universally played mobile genre worldwide. No cultural/language barrier.

### #3: C6 — Word Roguelite (daily-seed word duel)

**"Spell words to deal damage. Artifacts boost rare letters. Daily seed = same letters for everyone. How high can you score?"**

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Hook clarity | 4 | "Spell words, trigger combos, beat the boss" — clear but needs the number-explosion to land |
| Replayability | 4 | Combinatorial (letter draws × artifacts × word choices); daily seed renews |
| Retention | 4 | Daily-seed ritual + artifact unlock progression + ego-bait ("could you find this?") |
| Monetization | 4 | RV: extra letters, continue, artifact preview. Interstitial: between runs. Remove-ads. |
| UA/creative | 4 | CTR 3.5–5.5% [SPEC]; word + number-explosion is proven thumb-stop; "1% can beat Round 10" |
| Technical simplicity | 4 | Turn-based, text/tile logic, dictionary validation — Claude Code confident |
| Content lightness | 4 | Systemic (letter bags, artifact stats); dictionary is the "content" — open word lists exist |
| LiveOps lightness | 5 | Daily seed auto-generates |
| Originality | 3 | Word-roguelite is the most crowded Balatro-clone niche on Steam — mobile F2P slot is open but the concept isn't novel |
| Test suitability | 4 | Clean metrics but word-game audience skews older/smaller — need to test that the roguelite wrapper expands it |

**Total: 72/100 — STRONG**

**Whitespace:** Direct comp (Wordlike) is weak (~480 downloads, delisted snapshot). Letterlike is iOS-only premium. The F2P, mobile-native, daily-seed word roguelite has no scaled owner.

**Creative:** Honest CTR 3.5–5.5%. Spell-then-explode is filmable. Ego-bait ("could you find this word?") travels on TikTok. Share grid (Wordle-style) provides organic loop.

**Red-team fix applied:** Artifacts REWARD clever words (rare letters, long words), not replace spelling skill. Positioned as "daily word brawl," not "Balatro of words" — avoids direct comparison to the Steam flood.

**Key risks:**
- Steam word-roguelite flood means conventions are established — must meet expectations
- Skill-gating splits audience (wordsmith vs casual) — daily seed equalizes via shared letter pool
- Dictionary/locale minefield (profanity, regional spellings) — use open ENABLE list, Teen rating
- Anti-cheat vs word-solver apps on leaderboards

**International viability:** Strong in English-speaking markets (US/UK/AU/CA). Expandable to other Latin-alphabet languages but word games inherently cap non-English TAM. "Tier 2" = profitable in English markets.

### #4: C1 — Single-Anchor Daily (one hero puzzle + practice drawer)

**"One new puzzle every day. Keep your streak. Share your score grid. The free daily brain ritual."**

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Hook clarity | 4 | "Daily puzzle + streak" — clear but not exciting in one frame |
| Replayability | 3 | One daily = limited sessions; practice drawer adds replay but not variety |
| Retention | 5 | Daily ritual + streak + forgiveness = the highest-retention shape in mobile (Wordle proved it) |
| Monetization | 2 | IAA on 90-second sessions = ~1–2 impressions; RV hints fight the puzzle fun; remove-ads caps LTV |
| UA/creative | 2 | CTR 2.5–4% [SPEC]; unfilmable — the habit IS the product, not a moment. Organic-or-nothing. |
| Technical simplicity | 5 | Already built — existing GamesTest engines |
| Content lightness | 3 | Good dailies need quality gates (difficulty/uniqueness solver) — 3–4× work of the engine itself |
| LiveOps lightness | 5 | Self-resetting daily |
| Originality | 3 | Single daily puzzles are a known shape; the gap is standalone+IAA+Android vs NYT/LinkedIn |
| Test suitability | 3 | Retention signal takes 2–4 weeks to read; habit formation is slow to measure |

**Total: 60/100 — NEEDS DIFFERENTIATOR**

**Why it's borderline:** Highest-retention shape in mobile (proven by Wordle/Connections/NYT Mini) but weakest monetization and creative. Growth is organic-share-or-nothing. You already own the engines. It's the cheapest to ship but hardest to grow.

**Red-team fix applied:** Cut the 5-puzzle bundle to ONE hero daily (Connections-like grouping puzzle — highest virality, best generation-to-quality ratio) with streak attached. Other engines become a streak-optional practice drawer.

**International viability:** Universal (if word-based: English first, expandable). Connections-like grouping is more locale-expandable than pure word games.

## Recommended First-Test Portfolio

### Lead bet: C5 (Dice-Placement Roguelite)

The "tier 2" sweet spot: a dedicated niche (roguelite-curious mobile players) with high engagement, good LTV, and no need to out-spend Block Blast.

**Why:** Highest-probability paid UA winner. Best honest creative in the portfolio. Internationally maximum TAM. Fast to build (grid logic). But: competes on polish against 8-figure studios, so positioned as the "if creative tests clear 6%+ CTR, this is the scale play" option.

**Why:** Same daily-seed engine as C5 (shared architecture). English-market tier 2 play. If C5 validates the "daily roguelite" shape, C6 is the word-game expansion at marginal build cost.

- **C3** — Strong concept but honest creative can't carry paid UA; only viable via community/organic; build if C5 proves the daily-roguelite audience exists
- **C1** — Already built (GamesTest engines); instrument for retention data as a passive learning asset; doesn't need new build effort
- **C4, C7** — Regional plays; revisit only if a proven idle core (C3) or timing engine needs a themed re-skin

## What "Tier 2" Means for This Portfolio

## STRONG CLAIMS

1. **C5 (daily-seed dice) is the convergent top pick across all three panels** — best risk-adjusted bet for an internationally-focused, Claude-Code-buildable first test.
2. **The daily-seed overlay is the single highest-leverage design move** — it fixes C5's "thin Balatro clone" problem, provides the organic share loop, and creates an honest daily ritual without authored content.
3. **C2 is the strongest paid-UA play** but requires competing on polish against the most funded category on earth — treat it as the scale bet IF creative validates.
4. **C4/C7 are regional plays and should be shelved** for an internationally-focused founder; they can be revisited as themed re-skins of proven cores.
5. **Tier 2 ($1.5–3k/day) is achievable at 100–300k DAU** with honest IAA monetization — this doesn't require a moonshot.

## WEAK SPOTS

1. All CTR/IPM figures are [SPEC] — the pre-build concept test is the only real validation.
2. C5's "no incumbent" carries demand risk — the niche may be small because the audience is small.
3. Balatro-clone fatigue timing is a judgment call; re-check store density before committing.
4. C2's juice/polish requirement is real — competing with Block Blast on feel without their budget is the hidden cost.
5. The 6-week timelines assume Claude Code can deliver the game-feel layer (particles, animations, haptics) — this is the one area where Compose Canvas may hit limits vs a game engine.
6. "Whitespace ≠ demand" — the most-open concepts (C5/C6/C3) are open partly because the audiences are smaller; the most-crowded (C2) is crowded because demand is enormous.
