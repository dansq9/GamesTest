# Competitor / Whitespace Scan — 7 Concepts (July 2026)

## C1 — "Daily Ritual Suite" (multi-puzzle daily bundle)

**Direct incumbents.** The category leaders are portals, not indie apps:
- **NYT Games** — bundles Wordle, Connections, Spelling Bee, Mini/Full Crossword, Strands, Tiles, Sudoku under one streak/subscription. The reference implementation of the positioning [EB: Google Play/App Store]. Massive scale.
- **LinkedIn Games** — Queens, Tango, Zip, Crossclimb, Pinpoint, Patches, plus new **Wend** (2026) [EB: linkedin.com/games]. The *closest to the exact concept* (rotating daily logic/word puzzles, cross-game streak) but **embedded inside LinkedIn, login-walled, no standalone**, monetized as engagement not IAA. The concept is essentially "LinkedIn Games as a standalone honest-IAA Android app."
- **Puzzmo (Zach Gage / Hearst)** — daily bundle, unified daily, **iOS-first**; free with **Puzzmo Plus $3.99/mo** — subscription, not IAA [EB: editorandpublisher.com].

**Nearest neighbors.** Sudoku.com (Easybrain), Nonogram/Picross apps, Queens-clone sites, Wordle-unlimited sites, small clones like "Patches LinkedIn style Puzzle". No polished F2P Android bundle with a *unified cross-puzzle streak* found [EB: Play search].

**Whitespace verdict: PARTIALLY OPEN.** Mechanics individually CROWDED, but the specific combination — standalone, Android-first, IAA-monetized, unified-cross-puzzle-streak-with-forgiveness + share + weekly gauntlet — has **no strong dedicated incumbent on Google Play**. NYT is subscription+brand-gated; LinkedIn is login-walled; Puzzmo is iOS-first subscription. The gap is real but *thin* — the giants could enter Android hard at any time.

**Gap winner needs:** the unified cross-puzzle streak-with-forgiveness as the retention spine, plus genuinely offline, no-login, instant-play — the thing NYT (paywall) and LinkedIn (must open LinkedIn) both fail at. "5 puzzles, one streak, no account, free."

**Trademark hazards: HIGH.** Wordle/Connections/Spelling Bee/Strands are NYT marks; **NYT filed DMCA against hundreds of Wordle clones in 2024** (Reactle + forks) and enforces "-le" trade dress [EB: axios, npr]. The **green/yellow emoji share grid is Wordle's signature trade dress** — the spoiler-free-share *mechanic* is fine; the exact visual is the most litigated element. Queens/Tango/Zip/Crossclimb are LinkedIn names — avoid all of them.

## C2 — "Rising Tide" block puzzle

**Direct incumbents.** The base mechanic is the single most downloaded mobile category on earth:
- **Block Blast! (Hungry Studio)** — **~870M lifetime downloads, 368M in 2025, #1 most-downloaded worldwide 2024 AND 2025 and into 2026, ~70M DAU / 300M MAU, ~$600k–$1M/day ad revenue** [EB: udonis, maf.ad, businesswire, pocketgamer.biz]. IAA-first — exactly this founder's model, at uncontestable scale.
- Tetris Block Blast/Block Party (PlayStudios) + 60+ block-puzzle clones [EB: minireview].

**Nearest neighbors.** Woodoku, 1010!, Tetris. **No mainstream casual 8×8 title ships a rising-flood loss mechanic.** Rising-pressure exists in competitive Tetris lineage (garbage rows) but has NOT been transplanted into casual drag-and-place block-fit [negative search result — moderate confidence].

**Whitespace verdict: CROWDED (base) / PARTIALLY OPEN (twist).** You cannot out-Block-Blast Block Blast. The tide rule is a genuine, unclaimed differentiator — but the genre's audience chose it for zero-pressure relaxation; the drowning fail-state fights the reason the genre is #1. A design tension, not just a market gap.

**Gap winner needs:** tide as a **score-chase and daily-seed engine** ("how deep did you survive"), positioned as the arcade cousin of Block Blast, not a replacement. Differentiate hard on theme + tide; the grid mechanic is legally and competitively un-defensible.

**Trademark hazards: MEDIUM–HIGH.** "Block Blast" is a defended name family; "Tetris" is fiercely litigated (name AND trade dress). Original nautical brand required.

## C3 — "Draft-prestige idle"

**Direct incumbents.** **None scaled.** Only itch.io/niche precedents (PokAidle: idle + roguelite prestige modifiers; assorted "incremental+roguelite" tags) [EB: itch.io]. No scaled Play title welding a randomized 1-of-3 draft to idle prestige as described.

**Nearest neighbors.** (1) Prestige idles — Idle Slayer, Melvor, Antimatter Dimensions, **Realm Grinder** (fixed faction tree = the closest existing "prestige changes the rules" idea, but not a randomized draft). (2) Roguelite draft games — StS-likes, the Balatro wave — not idle. The *fusion* is un-owned.

**Whitespace verdict: PARTIALLY OPEN → arguably OPEN.** The most genuinely un-colonized concept of the seven on mobile F2P. Caveat: "no incumbent" partly reflects a connoisseur niche — the idle mass market wants predictable growth; randomized runs can frustrate min-maxers [SPEC].

**Gap winner needs:** drafts legible and swingy enough that runs feel distinct without breaking the offline-earnings loop; modifiers must visibly reshape *which numbers go up*. A design-risk bet, not a crowded-market bet; first-to-scale on Android is achievable solo.

**Trademark hazards: LOW, one policy landmine.** **Google Play's gambling policy**: Luck Be a Landlord was banned in 13 countries for *aesthetic* gambling despite no real-money mechanics [EB: gamedeveloper, kotaku]. Frame the draft as "choice," never "spin/gacha."

## C4 — "Chai Empire" idle tycoon

**Direct incumbents.** Both halves exist separately:
- **Coffee-idle loop (global):** Idle Cafe Tycoon (HolyCow), Idle Coffee Shop Tycoon (Codigames), Café Tycoon (SDG) — CROWDED, polished, well-funded [EB: Play].
- **Desi chai theme (occupied, thinly):** **Chai Wala CEO** (GameEon), **Chaiwala Tycoon** (DTGames), **Indian Chai Wala Simulator** (LeZit), **Chai Wala – PK Game Fun** — small/low-production sim/clickers, no polished truck-art idle-tycoon with events/diaspora monetization found (scale unverified) [EB: Play; installs not confirmed].

**Whitespace verdict: PARTIALLY OPEN.** Generic loop CROWDED; desi skin occupied but weakly. The edge is production quality + live-ops + cultural authenticity, not concept novelty.

**Gap winner needs:** Codigames-grade tycoon depth wearing authentic truck-art + real Ramadan/Eid events tuned for Gulf/diaspora windows. Theme alone won't win; founder-market fit (Pakistan-based) is the hard-to-copy advantage.

**Trademark hazards: LOW–MEDIUM.** Avoid real chai brands/chains; "Chai Wala CEO"-type names taken; truck-art motifs are cultural but don't copy a specific living artist; watch Play cloning detection vs Codigames/HolyCow layouts.

## C5 — "Dice-placement roguelite"

**Direct incumbents.** Balatro-with-dice is hot on PC/Steam; **the mobile F2P slot is essentially unfilled:**
- **Balatro** (north star): mobile Sept 2024 — **$1M week one, $9M+ IAP by Jan 2025, 5M+ copies, TGA Best Mobile Game** — but **premium**, not F2P [EB: Wikipedia, Play].
- **Dice A Million** — the leading Balatro-with-dice: PC Feb 25 2026, 90% positive (973 reviews); **mobile "coming," in optimization as of April 2026** [EB: Steam]. The most dangerous incoming competitor — but premium and not yet shipped mobile.
- **Slice & Dice** (tann) — dice roguelike on Android/iOS, free-demo + single unlock (premium model) [EB: toucharcade].
- **Knucklebones apps** (Knucklebones Tactical Dice PVP; knucklebones.io) — placement mechanic, no Balatro scoring/charm/ante layer [EB: Play].

**Nearest neighbors.** Luck Be a Landlord (**banned in 13 Play countries for gambling aesthetic** [EB: kotaku]), Dicey Dungeons, Astrea — the family skews premium/paid; almost none are F2P-mobile-native with RV + cosmetics.

**Whitespace verdict: PARTIALLY OPEN.** Mechanic fusion proven (Balatro/Dice A Million); on mobile it's premium-only or PC-only. **F2P, mobile-native, dice-grid + scoring-synergy + daily-seed ladder has no scaled incumbent.** The clock is loud: first-to-F2P-mobile is winnable *now*, mid-2026.

**Gap winner needs:** the **F2P daily-seed ladder as the social/retention hook** (Balatro and Slice & Dice are premium single-player; a free daily competitive seed is the un-owned wedge) + honest RV continues that don't feel pay-to-win. Grid placement (spatial) + scoring synergies is fresh enough to feel distinct from pure Balatro.

**Trademark hazards: HIGH — worst naming minefield of the set.** **Do NOT ship a product called "Knucklebones"** (indelible Cult of the Lamb association; the ancient game is public domain, the name association isn't worth the fight) [EB: fandom/BGG]. No Balatro trade dress (joker cards, ante naming, scoring UI look). **Play gambling policy is a live existential risk** for dice+scoring+reward aesthetics (LBAL precedent) — design reroll/reward UI to avoid slot-machine reads; pre-plan the ratings questionnaire.

## C6 — "Word roguelite"

**Direct incumbents.** 2025 was a Steam *flood* of word-roguelikes [EB: PC Gamer]; **mobile F2P slot weak/empty:**
- **Wordlike – Word Roguelike** (Roadtrip Games) — the closest direct competitor: Balatro-with-words, on Android AND iOS, but **tiny and weak**: an AppBrain snapshot showed ~480 downloads, 3.75★/~25 ratings, and "currently not available on Google Play" [EB: appbrain — point-in-time, may be stale]. Weak execution = an opening.
- **Letterlike** — Balatro×Scrabble, **paid iOS $4.99** [EB: droidgamers].
- **Word Play** (Thinky), **Scriver**, **Beyond Words** — PC/premium-leaning.

**Nearest neighbors.** Bookworm Adventures (the OG letters-as-combat ancestor — **no modern mobile heir**), SpellTower, Wordscapes. The Bookworm×Balatro fusion has no scaled mobile owner.

**Whitespace verdict: PARTIALLY OPEN.** Direct comp exists but small/low-rated/premium/iOS-first. **The F2P, mobile-native, daily-seed word roguelite is effectively un-won.** Winner = "the good, free, daily version of Wordlike."

**Trademark hazards: MEDIUM.** No "Scrabble" (Hasbro/Mattel, heavily enforced); no "-le" naming or Wordle emoji-grid trade dress; "Bookworm" is PopCap/EA. Use open word lists (ENABLE/SOWPODS-derived/public), not a licensed dictionary.

## C7 — "Gully cricket timing duel"

**Direct incumbents.** Cricket-timing batting is extremely CROWDED in India/diaspora, and "gully" is a used brand token:
- **Gully Cricket Game** (Games2Win) — **the exact name + theme + swipe-timing mechanic already exists** [EB: Play].
- **Stick Cricket Super League** (Stick Sports) — timing/tap batting, league progression [EB: Play].
- **WCC3 (Nextwave)** — "110M+ downloads, world's most-downloaded cricket franchise"; **Sachin Saga** (licensed Tendulkar) [EB: cricket.net.in].

**Nearest neighbors.** Real Cricket (Nautilus), Hitwicket, hyper-casual cricket tap games. The under-served combination: minimalist one-thumb arcade timing + honest bot/ghost async ladder — most incumbents are heavy sims or management games.

**Whitespace verdict: CROWDED (category) / narrow wedge.** Realistic outcome: a solid niche/diaspora title, not a category upset. The "gully" name angle is already taken.

**Gap winner needs:** timing feel + honesty as the brand — openly framed "Quick Match = you vs a seeded challenger ghost" with genuinely satisfying one-thumb timing, in a category full of fake-live PvP and pay-to-win sim bloat.

**Trademark hazards: MEDIUM–HIGH.** "Gully Cricket" name occupied; avoid IPL/PSL/ICC/team/player IP (Sachin Saga *licenses* its likeness); street framing is the safe lane. Avoid betting/fantasy framing (regulated in IN/PK/Gulf).

## STRONG CLAIMS
1. **Block Blast owns C2's base loop at uncontestable scale** (~870M lifetime, #1 worldwide 2024–26, IAA-first). C2 is only viable as a differentiated arcade cousin.
2. **C3 is the genuinely least-colonized concept on mobile F2P** — both parent genres proven, fusion un-owned. Highest novelty, lowest direct competition.
3. **The Balatro-with-dice/words waves are real but stuck on premium/PC** — C5 and C6 have no F2P-mobile-native leader; the free daily-seed lane is open in both. Dice A Million's mobile arrival narrows C5's window.
4. **NYT enforces Wordle IP aggressively** (mass DMCA takedowns, "-le" and emoji-grid trade dress) — constrains naming/share-UI in C1 and C6.
5. **Play's gambling policy is a demonstrated existential risk** for dice/slot aesthetics (LBAL evicted from 13 countries) — must be designed around in C5 and C3.
6. **"Gully Cricket" and chai-wala names are occupied**, and cricket timing is dominated by 100M+ franchises — C7's advantages are weaker than they appear.

## WEAK SPOTS
1. **No live Play install/rating counts pulled directly** (403s); all scale figures are press/aggregator. The **Wordlike ~480-download figure is a single, possibly stale AppBrain snapshot** — verify current status before treating C6 as wide open.
2. **C3's "no incumbent" is partly absence-of-evidence** — a scaled title could exist under naming the searches missed; and OPEN carries demand risk (idle mass market may reject run randomization) [SPEC].
3. **Dice A Million mobile timing/pricing is a moving target** — re-check before committing to C5.
4. **C4 desi-chai apps' quality/scale unverified** — install one before assuming the theme is winnable.
5. **C2's "no rising-tide precedent" is a negative result** — moderate confidence only.
6. **Puzzmo's Android status ambiguous** — affects how open C1 truly is on Android.
7. **Whitespace ≠ demand.** Openness and revenue potential are inversely correlated across this set: the most-open concepts (C3/C5/C6) are niche tastes; the most-crowded (C2/C7) are crowded because demand is enormous. Weigh accordingly.
