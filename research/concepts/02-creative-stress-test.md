# UA / Creative & Discovery Stress-Test — 7 Concepts (July 2026)

**Framing evidence [EB]:** Video is still ~81% of gaming creatives, but puzzle skews harder (~83.5% video) and playables carry the highest performance scores (191 in 2025) — hybrid video→playable is the fastest-growing format. Winning creatives lean on three psychological engines: **frustration/failure-bait** (Royal Match model), **oddly-satisfying resolution**, and **ego/competition ("only X% can")**. Fake-gameplay ads still out-CTR honest ones 4–5x, which is precisely the trap this founder is refusing — so the honest-creative constraint *lowers the CTR ceiling* on every concept and makes **thumb-stop-per-second efficiency** the whole game. Portfolio-relevant tells [EB]: 100% of winning playables use a guiding hand/arrow; ~80% use hook-text; cliffhanger/unresolved endings drive curiosity. ASO reality [EB]: head puzzle/idle keywords are saturated (247–250 apps on "idle tycoon …", 250 on "infinite block puzzle"); the winning move is long-tail specificity ("brain teaser for adults" lifted DLs 120%).

The honest-creative rule rewards concepts where **the real gameplay IS the satisfying/failure moment** and punishes concepts where the fun is invisible or slow (idle).

## C1 — Daily Ritual Suite (5 daily puzzles, unified streak)

**1. The clip.** Honestly, there isn't one good clip — there are five mediocre ones. Best attempt: a 6-second **emoji-grid share reveal** — thumb opens on a filled Wordle/Connections-style colored grid animating in with a streak counter ticking "Day 147," then a fast montage of the 5 mini-puzzles solving. Thumb-stop = the recognizable emoji-grid shape (borrowed equity from Wordle's cultural muscle memory).
**2. Hook fit.** Can honestly use: **progress-power** (streak), **satisfying** (grid fill), mild **"1% keep a 100-day streak."** CANNOT use failure-bait (no drown/loss drama) or near-miss. Narrative-outrage: none.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **2.5–4%**, mostly under the bar; IPM **5–10**. The suite is *unfilmable as one product* — five games in one frame reads as "I don't know what this is." Confidence: medium-high that paid underperforms.
**4. ASO/organic.** "daily puzzle," "word game," "brain games" — high competition but the **emoji-grid share is a genuine organic weapon** [EB: Wordle's entire growth was share-driven, zero paid]. This is the one concept where the share mechanic could substitute for a creative. Seasonal spikes: New Year "streak/habit" resolution window (Jan). TikTok clip-ability: **low** (nothing kinetic).
**5. Store page.** Icon: a single bold emoji-grid tile (green/yellow), no character. Screenshots: (1) the streak/calendar heatmap, (2) the 5 puzzle tiles as a "daily menu," (3) the shareable result card.
**6. Fatal risk.** Suites don't communicate in one frame; the algorithm can't categorize it; retention depends on habit that paid installs won't have. **Growth path is organic-or-nothing.**

## C2 — Rising Tide block puzzle (Block-Blast-like, water rises, drown = over)

**1. The clip.** 12s. First 3s: board already 60% flooded, water 2 rows from the top, a red "DROWNING" pulse — a hovering hand [EB tell] drops a piece that clears **three lines at once**, water crashes down 4 rows with a satisfying whoosh. Beat 2: near-death again. Beat 3: sandbag-revive RV moment shown honestly, then cliffhanger cut mid-recovery. Thumb-stop = **the rising water = literal ticking clock**, which Block Blast's static board lacks.
**2. Hook fit.** Best-in-portfolio hook coverage: **failure-bait** (drowning), **oddly-satisfying** (multi-line clear + water drop), **near-miss** (survive by one row), **progress-power** (push the tide back). This is the *only* concept that honestly hits the full Royal Match hook stack. CANNOT do narrative-outrage, doesn't need to.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **4.5–7%** (above bar); IPM **10–16**. Block puzzle is a **proven, algorithm-legible** category [EB: Block Blast 368M DLs 2025, No.1] and the water twist adds the drama the base genre lacks. Highest-confidence paid winner.
**4. ASO/organic.** "block puzzle," "block blast," "1010" — brutally saturated (250 apps), so you rank via twist long-tail ("rising water block puzzle," "flood block puzzle") + brute-force paid. TikTok clip-ability: **high** (satisfying clears are native TikTok content). No share-mechanic virality. Seasonal: none — evergreen.
**5. Store page.** Icon: bright blocks half-submerged in a blue waterline (instantly differentiates from every flat-block competitor). Screenshots: (1) water near top + "Clear lines before you drown!", (2) massive multi-line clear mid-splash, (3) sandbag revive.
**6. Fatal risk.** Category saturation means CPI is a knife-fight vs Block Blast's LTV-funded bids; you win on creative differentiation, not keyword. Risk that "drown = game over" reads as *stressful* to the relax-puzzle audience — test a "chill mode" creative variant.

## C3 — Draft-prestige idle (prestige = roguelite modifier draft)

**1. The clip.** This is where honest idle goes to die. Best attempt: 15s speed-ramp — a number-go-up montage, then the **prestige draft screen** where 3 run-warping cards are offered and one is picked, then the numbers explode 100x faster. Thumb-stop attempt = the *card-draft choice moment* (borrowed from roguelite/Balatro visual grammar), NOT the idle loop.
**2. Hook fit.** Can honestly use **progress-power** (explosive growth post-draft) and weak **satisfying**. CANNOT use failure-bait, near-miss, or "1%." The core idle loop is passive → **inherently not thumb-stopping** [EB: idle relies on *fake* ads 4–5x more than any genre].
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **2–3.5%**, below bar on honest footage; IPM **4–8**. The honest-creative rule is **maximally punishing** here. Confidence: high that honest idle underperforms paid.
**4. ASO/organic.** "idle," "incremental," "prestige," "roguelite idle" — 247+ apps, saturated. Roguelite-idle is a **discoverable niche on Reddit/Discord** (r/incremental_games) where honest depth-signaling actually converts — organic path is community, not ASO. TikTok: **low-medium**. No share mechanic.
**5. Store page.** Icon: a glowing card-draft fan over a rising graph. Screenshots: (1) the 3-card prestige draft, (2) an absurd number + multiplier stack, (3) a "build variety" collage.
**6. Fatal risk.** The thing that makes it good (deep, emergent, long-session) is invisible in a feed. Sell it to the ~50k-person incremental community by hand; do **not** expect paid to carry it.

## C4 — Chai Empire (chai-dhaba idle tycoon, truck-art, Ramadan/Eid, diaspora/Gulf)

**1. The clip.** 15s. First 3s: a hyper-colorful **truck-art dhaba** scene, chai pouring in a satisfying stream into a cutting-glass, a "+₹50" pop, then rapid franchise expansion across a stylized map with dhol-beat audio. Thumb-stop = **aesthetic novelty** — truck-art is a genuinely under-used, scroll-arresting visual language, not the mechanic.
**2. Hook fit.** **Progress-power** (empire growth), **satisfying** (chai pour — the one honestly filmable satisfying beat idle can own), light **narrative/aspiration** (dhaba→empire fantasy). CANNOT do failure-bait or near-miss. Still idle — the mechanic hook is weak, the **cultural/aesthetic hook is strong and differentiated**.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **3–5%** *within targeted diaspora/Gulf/desi audiences*; **2–3%** on broad T1. IPM **6–11** in-audience. Confidence: medium — hinges entirely on tight geo/culture targeting.
**4. ASO/organic.** **Best seasonal-discovery story in the portfolio** [EB: Ramadan/Eid are massive MENA/desi install + spend spikes]. Keyword clusters in Urdu/Hindi/Arabic ("chai wala," "dhaba tycoon," "chai empire") are **low-competition** vs English saturation — real ASO whitespace. TikTok/Reels: **high** in desi creator ecosystems (truck-art + chai content is native). Localized store listings = cheap organic edge. Event spikes: Ramadan (**~Feb 2027** is the target window), Eid, Independence Days (Aug 14/15).
**5. Store page.** Icon: an ornate truck-art chai glass, maximal color (pops in a grid of grey idle icons). Screenshots: (1) the dhaba with truck-art framing, (2) franchise map, (3) a Ramadan/Eid event screen with lanterns.
**6. Fatal risk.** Still idle → weak mechanic hook; carried by aesthetic, which fatigues. Cultural targeting **reads regional/niche to T1 algorithms** → cheap in-culture, expensive to escape the niche. Founder must nail Urdu/Arabic localization or the whole edge evaporates.

## C5 — Dice-placement roguelite (Knucklebones × Balatro, daily seed)

**1. The clip.** 12s. First 3s: a die drops into a grid, a combo chains and the score **multiplies with escalating chime SFX** (x2→x4→x9), charm icons glow as they trigger. Beat 2: an ante/boss appears with a scary target. Beat 3: near-miss clutch clear, cliffhanger cut. Thumb-stop = the **Balatro-style number-explosion + multiplier juice**, one of the most clip-native aesthetics of 2024–26 [EB: Balatro = 2024 GOTY, spawned a wave of dice/word imitators].
**2. Hook fit.** **Oddly-satisfying** (multiplier cascade), **progress-power**, **near-miss** (clutch vs ante), **"1% can beat Ante 8."** Strong, honest coverage.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **4–6%**; IPM **8–13**. Balatro visual grammar is proven thumb-stop and honestly filmable. Caveat: skews **hardcore/PC-roguelite** audience → smaller TAM, higher CPI than casual puzzle but higher LTV/retention. Confidence: medium-high.
**4. ASO/organic.** "dice game," "roguelike," "deckbuilder," "balatro-like" — moderate competition, and **"daily seed" enables an emoji/score-grid share** (Wordle-style leaderboard flex) — genuine organic loop. TikTok: **high** (roguelite "look at this run" clips are a native, thriving format). r/roguelites, r/balatro adjacency = free launch audience. Seasonal: none.
**5. Store page.** Icon: a glowing die on a grid with a multiplier "x9" burst. Screenshots: (1) mid-combo explosion, (2) charm/artifact loadout, (3) daily-seed leaderboard.
**6. Fatal risk.** Balatro-clone fatigue is real by mid-2026 [EB: "wave of imitators"] — differentiation pressure. Audience is niche vs casual puzzle → cheaper to reach on Discord/TikTok than to scale on paid. Complexity risk: too many charms shown = confusing first 3s.

## C6 — Word roguelite (Bookworm × Balatro, artifacts modify letter values)

**1. The clip.** 12s. First 3s: player spells a word, tiles light up, an artifact **triples the "Q" value** and the score detonates past a target with the same multiplier-juice as C5. Beat 2: escalating target/boss. Beat 3: clutch big word, cliffhanger. Thumb-stop = **word-forms-then-number-explodes** — Wordle familiarity + Balatro juice.
**2. Hook fit.** **Satisfying**, **progress-power**, **near-miss**, **"1% can spell their way past Round 10,"** plus **mild intellectual ego-bait** ("could you find this word?"). Honest and strong.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **3.5–5.5%**; IPM **7–12**. Slightly under C5 — word games skew narrower/older, and "watch me spell" is marginally less kinetic than dice cascades. Confidence: medium.
**4. ASO/organic.** "word game," "word puzzle," "anagram" — high competition BUT **word-roguelite is the single most-crowded Balatro-clone niche on Steam** [EB: PC Gamer 2025 survey of the wave] → validated demand, but crowded. **Daily-word share (emoji grid)** = strong organic loop. TikTok: medium-high ("could you find this word?" ego-bait travels). Seasonal: none.
**5. Store page.** Icon: a glowing letter tile with a multiplier burst. Screenshots: (1) word + score explosion, (2) letter-value artifact board, (3) daily challenge/share card.
**6. Fatal risk.** Most-crowded roguelite niche → differentiation is hard and late (mid-2026). Word games cap at a smaller, older paid audience. First-frame must foreground the *number explosion*, not the letters.

## C7 — Gully cricket timing duel (one-thumb batting vs bot ladder)

**1. The clip.** 10s. First 3s: bowler runs in on a vivid **street/gully backdrop**, ball hurtles at camera, one-thumb tap → **SIX**, crowd/gully roar, ball smashes a window or car. Beat 2: harder delivery, near-miss edge. Beat 3: "beat the bot" ladder cliffhanger. Thumb-stop = **ball-at-camera + timing tension + six** (visceral, universal sports-timing beat).
**2. Hook fit.** **Near-miss/timing** (native strength), **satisfying** (six + street destruction), **progress-power** (bot ladder), light **"1% can hit a perfect six."** Strong honest kinetic hook.
**3. Performance band [SPEC].** CTR **5–7% in cricket geos (India/Pak/Bangladesh/Gulf/UK-desi)**; **1.5–3% on broad T1 US** where cricket is illegible. IPM **8–14 in-geo**. Confidence: medium-high in-geo, high that it flops on untargeted T1.
**4. ASO/organic.** "cricket game," "gully cricket," "street cricket" — moderate competition in-region, and **massive seasonal-event discovery** [EB: cricket games spike hard around tournaments]. Align launch/burst to a marquee tournament window. TikTok/Reels: **very high** in South Asian ecosystems. No share mechanic, but creator/UGC potential is huge.
**5. Store page.** Icon: a bat mid-swing against a gully wall, ball streaking. Screenshots: (1) six with street destruction, (2) bot-ladder progression, (3) "Quick Match" one-thumb tutorial.
**6. Fatal risk.** **Reads regional to T1 algorithms** — CPI cheap in-geo but invisible/mistargeted on global auctions. In-geo IAA eCPMs are low → **UA math must survive $0.05–0.15 CPI against pennies of LTV**. Depth risk: can one-thumb batting sustain retention past the novelty six?

# RANKING

### Ranking A — Near-$0 budget (organic + ASO + micro-spend)
1. **C4 Chai Empire** — best seasonal (Ramadan/Eid) + non-English ASO whitespace + native desi TikTok/Reels virality.
2. **C7 Gully cricket** — enormous in-geo UGC/creator organic + tournament-timed free spikes.
3. **C5 Dice roguelite** — daily-seed share loop + community launch (r/roguelites/TikTok) nearly free.
4. **C6 Word roguelite** — emoji-grid share + validated (crowded) niche.
5. **C1 Daily Ritual Suite** — strong share mechanic, but unfilmable suite + habit-dependent retention = brutal cold-start without a following.
6. **C2 Rising Tide** — great creative but block puzzle is a paid-scale category; 250-app ASO knife-fight, no share loop.
7. **C3 Draft-prestige idle** — only the incremental community as on-ramp; starves without budget.

### Ranking B — Modest $2–5k test budget
1. **C2 Rising Tide** — highest honest CTR/IPM (full hook stack), algorithm-legible category, evergreen.
2. **C5 Dice roguelite** — proven Balatro thumb-stop grammar; higher LTV cushions CPI.
3. **C6 Word roguelite** — same engine, slightly lower kinetic ceiling, smaller TAM.
4. **C7 Gully cricket** — superb in-geo CTR; low in-geo eCPMs make CPI→LTV fragile; fine if you accept a regional business.
5. **C4 Chai Empire** — good in-audience CTR; idle mechanic caps IPM; diaspora TAM limits paid scale.
6. **C1 Suite** — sub-bar CTR; paid can't fix an unfilmable multi-product.
7. **C3 Draft-prestige idle** — honest idle CTR structurally below the 4% bar.

### Testable BEFORE building (fake-but-honest concept footage)
- **Easiest to fake honestly (mockup/AE, no engine):** C2 (animate a flooding block board), C5 & C6 (animate the multiplier explosion — mostly VFX), C7 (a single scripted six with ball-at-camera). All four can validate CTR/IPM on ~$500 click-tests **pre-build**.
- **Hard to fake honestly:** C4 & C3 (idle payoff is *time*, not a moment — an exciting clip risks becoming a "fake ad"). C1 is fakeable but the test measures curiosity, not the habit that drives its retention → **misleading signal**.

**Recommended sequence:** run $300–500 concept-footage click-tests on **C2, C5, C7** simultaneously *before writing engine code*; greenlight the winner. Keep C4 as a parallel organic/seasonal bet (build toward Ramadan 2027), not a paid bet.

## STRONG CLAIMS
- **The honest-creative rule is a hard filter that C3 (and largely C1/C4) cannot pass on paid** — idle/suite fun is invisible or slow; the genre's real UA engine is the 4–5× fake ads the founder refuses. Don't fund idle with paid.
- **C2 is the highest-probability paid winner**: the only concept that honestly stacks failure-bait + oddly-satisfying + near-miss inside a proven, algorithm-legible category.
- **C2, C5, C6, C7 are all testable BEFORE building** with cheap honest concept footage — run those click-tests first as go/no-go gates.
- **C4 and C7 are geo/season plays, not global paid plays** — cheap and viral in-culture, structurally mistargeted and CPI-fragile on T1 auctions.
- **Share/daily-seed loops (C1, C5, C6) are the only near-$0 growth substitutes for a creative.**

## WEAK SPOTS
- All CTR/IPM bands are **[SPEC]**, reasoned from category benchmarks, not account data — real numbers move ±2 CTR points on execution alone.
- **eCPM/LTV assumptions for C4/C7 (India/desi/Gulf IAA) are directional, not measured.**
- **Balatro-clone fatigue timing (C5/C6)** is a judgment call — verify current store roguelite density before committing.
- No precise ASO difficulty scores (only app-count proxies); validate long-tail whitespace (esp. C4 non-English) in a real ASO tool.
- **C2's "drown = stress"** risk to the relax-puzzle audience is asserted, not tested — include a chill-mode creative variant in the first test batch.
