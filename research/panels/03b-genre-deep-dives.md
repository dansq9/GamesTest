# A. BOARD-EVENT / COIN-LOOTER (Monopoly GO, Coin Master, Dice Dreams, Board Kings)

## Corrections to common premises
- **SuperPlay (Dice Dreams, Domino Dreams) was acquired by Playtika** — $700M upfront + up to $1.25B earnout (announced Sept 18 2024, closed Nov 20 2024) [Playtika/GlobeNewswire]. Not Take-Two/Zynga.
- **Board Kings is by Jelly Button Games** (Tel Aviv, also Pirate Kings — the title credited with originating the coin-looter subgenre), acquired by **Playtika in 2017**. Net: Playtika owns *two* coin-looter studios — genre consolidation under one public company.

## Monopoly GO (Scopely)
- Launched April 2023. $1B by Nov 2023; $2B by ~March 2024; $2.5B consumer spend in 2024 alone (biggest-spending mobile game of 2024, Sensor Tower); $5B at just under two years [Forbes May 2025]; **$6B in 2025 — fastest mobile game in history to that milestone (1,275 days)**, beating Monster Strike (1,673) and Honor of Kings (1,762); #14 all-time global mobile revenue [Sensor Tower/Scopely Aug 2025]. Run-rate ~$200M/month; Scopely projects $8B lifetime by summer 2026.
- Full-year IAP: **$1.43B in 2024, $1.36B in 2025** (slight decline) [AppMagic Monetization Report 2025 via Mobidictum/GameDev Reports].
- Costs: ~$70M dev over ~7 years pre-launch; ~$500M marketing in first 10 months (<25% of revenue); **$1B+ lifetime marketing by March 2025**; ~$2.5M/day UA at points in 2024 [PocketGamer.biz/Kotaku].
- Team: **150+ dedicated staff** across 5 countries inside ~2,400–3,000-person Scopely [mobilegamer.biz].
- Loop: dice (capped energy) → board movement → build/upgrade → sticker albums (9/set; gold stickers tradeable only in Golden Blitz windows) → trade → repeat; minigames (Cash Grab, Prize Drop); partner-IP events (Hello Kitty, Wizarding World, Simpsons). Attracted +1,100% overindex of board-game players vs general population [DoF/GameRefinery].

## Coin Master (Moon Active)
- Launched 2016. **$6B+ lifetime as of ~Aug 2024** [Sensor Tower; GWO; PocketGamer.biz]; ~$1.2B/year since 2020. Title IAP: $697M (2024), ~$650M (2025) [AppMagic] — flat/healthy post-Monopoly-GO; **Monopoly GO expanded the category rather than cannibalizing it** [DoF July 2025]. Geo: US 39%, Germany 11%.
- **Discrepancy flagged:** Moon Active company-wide revenue reported "$2B+ in 2024" [Jewish Business News Jan 2025] vs title-IAP $697M — likely methodology (company bookings incl. ads vs store-IAP tracking); unreconciled. Moon Active laid off dozens (~5%) Jan 2025 despite record results. Team ~1,900–2,500.
- Loop: spins (energy) → 3-reel slot → coins / attack / raid / shield → village upgrades (5 buildings × 5 stars) → next village; PvP attack/raid; shields (max 3).
- Moon Active's answer to Monopoly GO: **Coin Master – Board Adventure** (dice-board spinoff), global 2025, ~$250K/day at launch [PocketGamer.biz/Gamigion].

## Dice Dreams (SuperPlay → Playtika)
- **$400M+ lifetime** [Sensor Tower]; $125M in ~3 years pre-Monopoly-GO, then +$275M in ~1 year after — Monopoly GO's UA grew the whole category. 2025: **$134M** (AppMagic) — 3rd-largest coin looter behind Monopoly GO ($1.3B) and Coin Master ($650M); **top 3 = ~90% of category revenue**. Recent decline: ~$9M/mo GP (Dec 2024) → ~$4M/mo iOS (Feb 2026). Dice Dreams + Domino Dreams combined ADAU ~1.7M (Aug 2024).

## Board Kings (Jelly Button → Playtika)
- Current ~$300K/month combined, ~26K monthly downloads — small and declining. No verified historical peak figure found.

## Newer entrants ("roll-and-earn" wave)
- Carnival Tycoon, Animals & Coins, Top Tycoon (#6 grossing coin looter end-2025), MONOPOLY Bingo!. 4 new games broke the coin-looter top-10 in 2025 even as category revenue slipped (~$2.4B, −3% YoY; downloads +11%; RPD $29→$27) — maturing, contested.

## Shared architecture
- Core loops "almost identical" (DoF); **LiveOps depth is the differentiator**: Monopoly GO runs **40 concurrent event slots vs Coin Master's 9** [Naavik/AppMagic]; "Reward Flow" chains one event's payout into the next event's entry. A single well-designed LiveOps event can lift revenue up to 55% [AppMagic/GamerBraves]; LiveOps-equipped titles ~30× revenue of non-LiveOps [Sensor Tower via GWO 2023]. Collection/albums with rarity-gated trading windows; PvP sabotage monetizing defense; partner-IP FOMO events; interconnected event currencies ("circular economy", GameRefinery — pattern copied by Royal Match, Gossip Harbor, Match Factory).
- Coin looters = ~5.14% of ALL 2024 mobile IAP spend, fastest-growing subgenre of 2024 [Sensor Tower SoMG 2025]. **Board Games category: >90% of revenue from titles older than 2 years** — extreme incumbency lock-in.

## Solo/lean-team viability — VERDICT: NOT VIABLE at competitive scale
- Monopoly GO team 150+, ~$70M dev, $1B+ marketing; Moon Active ~2,000 staff; Playtika paid ~$1.95B potential consideration just to BUY into the genre; GameRefinery: running a casual game requires "a medium-sized team dedicated to LiveOps and relentless A/B testing"; top-3 hold ~90% share; **no solo/small-indie counter-example found 2024–26**.
- Instructive contrast: solo devs ARE winning in adjacent "gambling-aesthetic" space — **Balatro** (solo, 5M+ copies, GOTY awards) and **Buckshot Roulette** (8M+ players) — but as premium/ad-light roguelikes that explicitly do NOT adopt the F2P LiveOps/PvP coin-looter model. The industry's revealed verdict: lean teams win in this design space by *not* building the coin-looter genre as defined.

# B. TOWER DEFENSE (2025–26)

## Growth status
- No clean TD-genre YoY figure exists from tier-1 trackers (reported under "Strategy"). **Strategy is 2025's strongest-growing major genre (+16–18% revenue) but the growth is 4X-survival (Last War, Whiteout Survival), not TD** [PocketGamer.biz/AppMagic; Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026]. TD is explicitly framed as a *hybridization ingredient* other genres borrow ("tower defense + idle", "RPG + simulation") [Business of Apps/AppMagic 2025].
- Pure TD: mature/niche-stagnant; vitality only at hybrid edges. SEO market-mill "TD market size" reports are wildly inconsistent ($937M→$23B ranges) — noise, not signal.

## Top-grossing TD now
- **Rush Royale (My.Games)**: category leader — $370M/90M dl (Dec 2024) → $470M+/100M+ (Dec 2025) → **$500M lifetime (~June 2026)** [PocketGamer]. PvP + randomized draws, not authored PvE.
- **Kingdom Rush (Ironhide, ~51–53 staff)**: 60M+ mobile downloads; 2025: Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance (July) + Kingdom Rush Battles PvP spinoff (Oct, "cautiously optimistic" reviews, complaints re: connectivity/ad load). No consolidated revenue found.
- **Bloons TD 6 (Ninja Kiwi, ~80 staff)**: ~$800K/month combined (Feb–Apr 2026 est.); Ninja Kiwi FY2023 NZ$89.5M revenue / NZ$40.8M net profit.
- **Random Dice (111%)**: faded to ~$100K/mo iOS US (Nov 2025).
- **Arknights (Hypergryph)**: TD-RPG-gacha, ~$248–355M/yr, >$1B lifetime (directional); growth energy now in **Arknights: Endfield** (open-world ARPG, $173M in 2 weeks Jan 2026, 60–70% PC/console) — even Arknights' studio sees limited pure-mobile-TD upside.
- **Warcraft Rumble (Blizzard)**: the cautionary big-budget flop — monthly revenue −92% in year 1 ($24.6M→$2M); ~$74M lifetime; content development ENDED July 2025, maintenance mode [PocketGamer.biz].
- Mid-tier: Kingdom Guard (merge+TD, ~$800K/mo, late-lifecycle), Galaxy Defense (Apr 2025), Penguin GO! TD (brief US top-200), **Kingshot (Century Games)** — 4X using a TD onboarding hook, $500M+ in 11 months (TD absorbed into the growing genre, not TD itself).

## Authored vs procedural/PvP
- Authored PvE remains the backbone of evergreens (BTD6, Kingdom Rush); **essentially all new commercial energy is procedural/PvP/roguelite** (Rush Royale, Random Dice, KR Battles, Wittle Defender, Isle of Arrows, Emberward). Roguelite elements break "solved meta" syndrome.

## TD hybrids trending
- **TD + roguelite is the standout 2025 story**: **Wittle Defender (Habby)** — $9.7M first month, $10M+ in ~2 months, US iOS top-200 [PocketGamer.biz/GameRefinery]. TD+merge real but smaller (Kingdom Guard); long tail of unproven merge-TD clones.

## Small-team successes
- **Isle of Arrows** (solo dev Daniel Lutz, ex-Square Enix Montreal): tile-placement roguelike TD, iOS/Android/Steam, ~$214K lifetime Steam est.
- **Emberward** (solo, Taiwan): tetromino-maze roguelite TD, 96% positive, 100–200K Steam owners est.
- **Core Defense** (small team): ~$20K first week. **Tangy TD** (solo, 2026): $30K day one / $250K first week — Steam, not mobile.
- Context: Ironhide (~51) and Ninja Kiwi (~80) — the most durable "independent" TD studios — are real studios with live-ops, sustaining mid-tier (not top-10) grossing.

## Verdict
- **Solo/lean IS realistic** for premium or light-F2P PvE roguelite/authored-hybrid TD with no live backend (Isle of Arrows/Emberward model → low-to-mid six figures, often cross-platform Steam+mobile).
- **Top of mobile grossing requires a studio**: PvP matchmaking, anti-cheat, balance patching, gacha economies, cross-server events. Warcraft Rumble proves budget alone doesn't save you; live-ops iteration speed matters.

# C. IDLE / TYCOON + CASUAL RACING (2025–26)

## Idle — top games
- **Legend of Mushroom (Joy Net)**: $270M lifetime IAP by ~May 2024, ~$2.5M/day peak; KR+JP ≈ 70% of earnings [Sensor Tower via WN Hub; DoF Apr 2024].
- **AFK Journey (Lilith)**: $158M by ~6 months, $185M+ lifetime [MAF/PocketGamer.biz]; project team ~190 FTE inside ~2,300-person Lilith. (A "5.012B yuan 2024" figure circulating is likely misattributed/company-wide — unconfirmed.) AFK Arena: $1.5B+ lifetime.
- **Capybara Go! (Habby)**: $100M in ~3 months (Feb 2025), peak $33.3M/mo Dec 2024 (~$850K/day IAP + $150K/day IAA); KR 28%, TW 17%. **By Sept 2025 weekly revenue ~$438K (~$1.9M/mo) — >90% decline from peak in 9 months.**
- **Idle Zombie Miner (Royal Ark)**: 17M+ installs, ~$23M H1 2025; grew through 2025 (live-ops execution, not genre, drives trajectory).
- **Idle Heroes (DH Games, 2016)**: still ~$1.3M/mo combined in late 2025 — idle's long-tail durability.
- Idle-tycoon × casual-casino hybrids: Top Tycoon (US top-100 grossing, peak #88), Carnival Tycoon (top-60) [GameRefinery Nov/Dec 2025]. MapleStory Idle RPG (Nexon): ~$37M/first month, #31 US grossing (Nov 2025).
- **SayGames idle-arcade portfolio**: $300M+ cumulative across 150+ games (My Perfect Hotel, Dreamdale, etc.) — portfolio economics, not single-hit.
- Market-mill sizing (directional only): idle ~$14.2B (2025); mix 54.7% IAP / 28.3% ads.

## Idle — lean-team verdict: CONTESTED
- **Naavik ("What Happened to Idle Games?")**: genre "cooling significantly"; saturation; reskins rarely break out; idle front-loads monetization without deep meta long-tails.
- 2025's chart-topping "idle" winners are all studio-backed (Lilith ~190 FTE on one title; Habby $1B+ publisher; Royal Ark took outside investment). Historic lean stories (Kolibri from a dorm → 100 people by Ubisoft exit 2020; East Side Games "3–5 people" claim, 2022) are pre-2024.
- Solo proof-point in the window: **Magic Research** (solo, ~$400K in 12 months) — but premium PC/Steam incremental, not mobile F2P.
- **Net: dev-cost genuinely low; a lean team CAN ship a profitable niche idle. WINNING charts now needs UA + localization (KR/JP/CN) + live-ops cadence. Naavik's saturation critique holds at the top; "cheap to build" holds at the bottom.**

## Racing/driving — top games
- **CarX (CarX Technologies, ~85 staff)**: 2024 better than 2023; no 2025 mobile figure found.
- **Traffic Rider / Traffic Racer (Soner Kara, SOLO dev, Turkey)**: Traffic Rider ~500K dl/~$40K per month (Dec 2025); 50M+ lifetime installs — **the strongest confirmed solo success in casual driving, but a legacy 2016 title coasting**, not a 2024–26 breakout.
- **Race Master 3D (SayGames)**: $1.3M IAP in all of 2024; ~4M downloads/month — classic hypercasual: huge volume, thin direct revenue, cross-promo vehicle.
- **Car Parking Multiplayer (solo-founded, Kazakhstan, 2017)**: 100M+ lifetime, ~$200K/mo — between hypercasual and midcore-sim (multiplayer + roleplay + customization).
- **Hill Climb Racing 2 (Fingersoft)**: ~$600K/mo, $88.3M lifetime IAP, franchise 2.5B installs; founded solo in a bedroom (2012), now 118 staff — lean-founded, scaled beyond lean.
- **CSR Racing 2 (Zynga/Take-Two): ~half of ALL mobile racing revenue** [GameRefinery] — extreme concentration.
- Hybridcasual top-10 lists (AppMagic Q1–Q3 2025): **no racing/driving title appears** — racing is a stable/legacy performer, not a growth category. UA context: hypercasual median CPI ~$0.40 Android / $1.50–2.50 iOS; NA gaming CPI $1.68 (+31% YoY).

## Racing — verdict
- Lean-team *buildable* (Traffic Rider proves the shape) but **not lean-team *winnable* fresh in 2025–26 UA economics**; no new solo breakout found in-window; sim end needs studios (licensing, physics, live-ops); **revenue ceiling structurally lower than idle** (best racing ~$600K/mo vs idle's $33M/mo peaks).

# D. PARTY / MINIGAME + MERGE (2025–26)

## Party / minigame collections
- **Stumble Guys** (Kitka, 8–9-person Finnish studio at build → Scopely 2022): 86% of "party royale" downloads (2022), 175M downloads that year, ~$500K/day Oct 2022; now ~38M MAU / ~3.6M DAU; ~$400K/mo US iOS (Mar 2026 snapshot, US-only). No global 2025 figure found. Peaked ~$70M (2022), declining since.
- **Eggy Party (NetEase)**: $750M lifetime mobile-storefront spend (Dec 2025); revenue peaked $395.7M (2023) → $158.7–183M (2024, sources conflict: PG.biz $183M vs Naavik $129M — both reported) → $158.7M (2025); >96% of revenue from China; 500M+ registered; UGC workshop 100M+ maps / 10M+ creators.
- **Fall Guys mobile (Epic)**: no standalone revenue/DAU disclosed anywhere; weakest of the three.
- "Party Star" as a Fall-Guys-style title: does not exist in analyst tracking (only a voice-chat social app of that name).
- **Verdict: prototype-buildable (Kitka proves it), not solo-sustainable.** Real-time sync for up to 32 players, regional server fleets, elastic matchmaking, anti-cheat, and a permanent minigame/UGC content treadmill; every winner is now AAA-parented (Scopely/NetEase/Epic). Indie backend costs $100–500/mo at low traffic → $1–5K+/mo; LiveOps 15–25% of first-year budget.

## Merge
- **Gossip Harbor (Microfun, merge-2)**: 2025's merge king — 9th top-grossing mobile game worldwide (Oct 2025); ~$62M/month IAP peak (highest ever in Merge-2); lifetime $677M–723M+ (sources differ); DAU ~5.4M; ~40% US spend; **LiveOps scaled from ~20 to ~100 events/month** [AppMagic/Mobidictum].
- **Travel Town**: $291M in 2025, ~$45M/mo by early 2026. **Developer = Magmatic Games (UK), publisher/majority owner = Moon Active** (since 2022) — not Space Ape (separate Supercell London studio; unrelated).
- **Seaside Escape (Microfun)**: $249.3M in 2025 — Microfun holds 2 of the top 3.
- **Merge Mansion (Metacore → Supercell)**: $500M (Aug 2024) → $600M+ (Apr 2025) → $700M+ (Sept 2025) lifetime; 2024 revenue €154M (−5% YoY), first-ever annual profit €18.7M; **peaked 2022–23, in sustained contraction**; Metacore laid off 159, closed DE/SE studios, Supercell completing full acquisition — PocketGamer.biz: "Is Metacore a warning sign?" (A circulating "$627.5M in 2025 +147%" figure is internally inconsistent with lifetime data — discarded as unverified.)
- **EverMerge**: $350M+ lifetime; acquired by JetSynthesys Jan 2026 (ownership chain Small Giant/Zynga vs "Big Fish" seller unreconciled). **Merge Dragons**: legacy Zynga "forever franchise," share fell 40%→13% (2019→2021); no 2025 figure — continued erosion likely.
- Genre status: Puzzle category $14.4B (+14%) with **downloads −3%** — deeper monetization of a flat base = maturing. **Merge growth is concentrated almost entirely in Gossip Harbor** (~60% of YoY puzzle-genre growth per Sensor-Tower-adjacent commentary); Merge2 category ~$600M (2022) → ~$950M+ (2024). Naavik (Jan 2026): match-3 & merge remain long-term winners — scale, durability, engaged audience intact.
- **Content treadmill:** Gossip Harbor 20→100 events/month; Merge Mansion event calendar nearly doubled Apr 2024→Mar 2025 — an events arms race just to defend share. Requires economy/LiveOps designers, continuous art, narrative writers.
- **Verdict: merge-2/3 is small-team BUILDABLE** (no netcode/matchmaking; fundamentally single-player/async; Metacore itself started small; Futureplay's Merge Gardens recovered as a smaller-team project) — **but top-of-charts competition is not lean-viable** (top 3 capture 90%+ of subgenre revenue; events arms race). Lean teams target the long tail below the top 3, not genre leadership.
