# SIMULATED 6-PLAYER PANEL — IAA-FIRST MOBILE GAME CONCEPT TEST (July 2026)

**EVIDENCE KEY:** `[EB: source]` = grounded in cited real data/reviews; `[PU]` = plausible-unverified persona inference. Web tools worked; personas' voices are anchored to review/survey evidence where the games they play are documented. Scores are simulated analytic judgments, not fielded data.

**Macro anchors used throughout:** Rewarded ads beat interstitials ~4:1 in preference; 62% call rewarded the most engaging format, ~95% completion, and rewarded-engagers retain up to 3.5x and convert to IAP up to 4.5x higher `[EB: MAF, Netmarvel 2025]`. One exposure to a *disruptive* ad feature raises churn 6–7%, and ads on reward/end-card screens **triple** quit rates; casual gamers are 30–50% more churn-prone after a disruptive ad `[EB: Deloitte/Google AdMob 2025]`. 72% of devs now run hybrid (IAA+IAP), which lifts ARPU ~28% over ad-only `[EB: Netmarvel/PocketGamer 2025]`. 2024 benchmarks: top-quartile D1 25–33%, D7 7–8%, D30 <3%; median session 5–6 min, median daily playtime 22 min `[EB: GameAnalytics 2024]`. Forced account creation loses ~23% of users and cuts first-run retention 30–60% `[EB: Corbado]`. In emerging markets, −10MB APK ≈ +2.5% install conversion; keep initial build ≤20–25MB `[EB: Google Play/Tolomei]`.

## (a) PER-PERSONA PROFILES

### 1. Bilal — 24, Karachi delivery rider (Redmi 4GB, limited data, never paid)
**Install triggers:** Real gameplay clips of Ludo/Carrom/board or fast action he can recognize; "works on low-end / small size" signals; friend/WhatsApp share. Store rating and a <30MB badge matter more than polish `[EB: Google Play/Tolomei]`. **First-session & abandon:** Time-to-fun must be near-instant; he plays in 3–8 min delivery gaps, so a session must *complete* a loop fast `[EB: GameAnalytics]`. Download-size sensitivity is his #1 gate — a 150MB+ APK or a large post-install download over mobile data = abandon before first play `[EB: emerging-market APK data]`. Free Fire's ~300MB/2GB-RAM tuning is his mental baseline `[EB: Sportsdigest]`. Forced login = suspicion + data cost; wants guest play. Heat/battery drain from heavy 3D = uninstall. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2 = a daily reward/streak he can grab on data; D7 = async social (beat a friend's Ludo/Carrom score); D30 = ranked/ladder standing among friends. **Ad tolerance:** Rewarded = welcome, his only "currency" since he won't pay cash `[EB: rewarded-preference]`; banners = fine; interstitials = tolerated only if short and not mid-match — data-heavy video interstitials on a metered plan annoy him. **Payment reality:** Effectively zero cash; has Easypaisa but treats games as free `[PU]`. Rewarded ads are how he "pays." **Social:** High — invites, async leaderboards, friend challenges are the whole point (Ludo/Carrom are social) `[EB: Ludo/Carrom social framing]`. **Top uninstall triggers:** Large size/data burn; rigged-feeling RNG or gem pay-to-win (mirrors Ludo Star "rigged dice / gems-to-win / L0 vs L260 matchmaking" complaints `[EB: MouthShut, Play reviews]`); overheating.

### 2. Sarah — 36, Ohio mom of two (iPhone, Block Blast + Royal Match, ~$5 IAP, hates fake ads)
**Install triggers:** Cozy, clean, "relaxing" puzzle aesthetic; honest gameplay footage; App Store editorial/rating trust. She is exactly the Block Blast wind-down player. **First-session & abandon:** Wants immediate calm competence — a satisfying clear in the first minute. Long tutorials bore her; she'll tolerate ~1 min of interactive teaching. **Her defining abandon trigger is ad density and deception:** Block Blast's own top complaint is that excessive ads make it "more frustrating than relaxing" `[EB: MAF]`, and she actively resents "save the king"-style bait. Notably Royal Match's advertised mini-games *do* exist in-game `[EB: MAF]` — she rewards games whose ads are honest and punishes Gardenscapes-style bait (ASA-banned 2020) `[EB: MAF]`. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2 = gentle streak/lives refill; D7 = new levels + light meta (decorate/collect); D30 = seasonal events done tastefully, not pay-walled. **Ad tolerance:** Rewarded = fine and chosen (extra moves/lives); banner = tolerable; **interstitial after every level = uninstall-class** and her single loudest grievance `[EB: Block Blast reviews]`. **Payment reality:** Occasional $5 — remove-ads, life bundles, or a cosmetic; will pay to *stop* ad pain, which makes ad restraint a monetization lever, not a sacrifice. **Social:** Low-moderate — light leaderboards/teams okay; resists spend-pressure social. **Top uninstall triggers:** Interstitial spam; fake-ad bait; hard paygates that stall progress (Royal Match "pay to pass" complaints `[EB: AppGamer]`).

### 3. Hamza — 18, Lahore student (mid-range Android, Survivor!.io-likes + Free Fire, TikTok discovery, watches ads, never pays)
**Install triggers:** TikTok/YouTube Shorts of real, flashy action-progression; "number-go-up" power fantasy; viral trend audio. Discovery is social-video-first, mirroring Block Blast's TikTok-driven 368M-download rise `[EB: MAF]`. **First-session & abandon:** Very low patience for slow starts; wants build-defining upgrades within the first run. Tolerates size better than Bilal but still hates 1GB+ installs on mid-range. Forced login = friction he'll skip. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2 = daily double-gold/energy; D7 = new weapon/evolution tiers, grind targets (survivor-likes retain via high upgrade costs = a reason to keep playing `[EB: MiniReview]`); D30 = events/leaderboards, new maps. **Ad tolerance:** **Rewarded is his native currency** — revive, 2x gold, chest, shop-refresh; he'll watch many per session gladly `[EB: MiniReview survivor-like monetization]`. Banner = ignored. Interstitials tolerated if occasional (survivor-likes do run rare forced ads on level-up `[EB: MiniReview]`), but aggressive forced ads feel cheap. **Payment reality:** Zero cash — he is a pure ad-monetized user; his value is impressions + UA virality, not IAP. **Social:** Moderate-high — leaderboards, guilds, shareable clips; competes for status not spend. **Top uninstall triggers:** Hard cash paywall that gates power (pay-to-win kills his free-rider identity `[PU]`); forced ads with no reward; heavy build.

### 4. Marcus — 42, German commuter (idle + Bloons TD6, buys remove-ads, values offline)
**Install triggers:** "Playable offline," deep systems, a visible one-time remove-ads/premium option; genre credibility (tower defense, idle-management). **First-session & abandon:** Willing to invest in a real tutorial *if* the systems are deep — opposite of the casual crowd. **His hard line: the 2nd unskippable interstitial = instant uninstall,** and always-online is a dealbreaker (train = intermittent signal) `[PU, consistent with BTD6 offline appeal EB: Fandom]`. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2 = offline idle accrual to collect; D7 = new towers/tiers/prestige; D30 = deep meta-progression and content updates (why BTD6 retains for years `[EB: OldCynic]`). **Ad tolerance:** Rewarded = acceptable pre-purchase (double offline earnings), and BTD6-style *optional* ads for rerolls are the model he respects `[EB: Bloons Wiki]`. Banner = tolerable. **Interstitial = the enemy; forced/unskippable = churn.** He resolves ad tension by *buying it away.* **Payment reality:** **Reliable low-ticket IAP — reflexive remove-ads + occasional premium/starter.** He is the hybrid model's proof case: pay-to-remove-friction. Remove-ads must actually remove *all* forced ads or he feels cheated. **Social:** Low — solo commuter play; leaderboards ignored, no invites. **Top uninstall triggers:** Forced online; 2nd unskippable interstitial; energy timers that block a train session; remove-ads that doesn't fully work.

### 5. Priya — 29, Dubai Indian expat (Monopoly GO + Coin Master, event-driven, family WhatsApp, $10–30/mo peaks)
**Install triggers:** Social/event fantasy — "play with your family," teams, tournaments, collectible sets; something she can pull the WhatsApp group into. **First-session & abandon:** Onboarding must reach the *social/collection* hook fast; a purely solo game feels empty to her regardless of polish. Tolerates login if it enables social (she *wants* to connect friends), unlike the others. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2 = daily spins/energy + event timer; D7 = partner/team events and family competition (Coin Master leans on Facebook-connected friends for spins/raids `[EB: Coin Master]`); D30 = live-ops seasonal events — the entire retention engine. **Ad tolerance:** Rewarded = fine (extra spins/energy). Interstitial = tolerated between event rounds. Banners = fine. Ads are secondary to her event/collection loop. **Payment reality:** **Real spender, $10–30/mo in event peaks** — event bundles, dice/spin packs, catch-up offers. She responds to FOMO and social-competitive pressure (Monopoly GO partner/tournament spend-pressure is a documented complaint `[EB: reddit-themed, PU]`). **Social:** **Maximal — invites, teams, family leaderboards are the product.** A dead friend graph = she churns. **Top uninstall triggers:** No social/event layer; predatory pressure she notices as manipulative; a game her family won't join (network-effect failure).

### 6. Linda — 55, UK (solitaire + word games, tolerates banners, anti-login/permissions, fixed daily habit)
**Install triggers:** Familiar solitaire/word imagery, "free, simple, no sign-up," large clear UI; trusts genre and rating over novelty. **First-session & abandon:** Wants to play *immediately* with no account. **Forced login or intrusive permission requests = instant distrust and abandon** — she never links accounts (mirrors MS Solitaire "can't play offline, asks for Microsoft account" friction `[EB: Ten Forums/HN]`). Long tutorials unnecessary; she knows solitaire. **Return D2/D7/D30:** D2/D7/D30 all driven by a stable **daily habit at fixed times** — daily deals, a daily crossword/solitaire challenge, quiet streaks. Consistency > novelty. **Ad tolerance:** **Banners = accepted** (she's the rare banner-tolerant player); rewarded = will use occasionally for a hint if clearly optional; interstitials = irritating but she'll endure short ones; **gambling/casino ads = offensive and trust-breaking** (a documented solitaire complaint `[EB: HN]`). **Payment reality:** Low — perhaps a one-time remove-ads if she trusts the developer and it doesn't require an account; suspicious of subscriptions. **Social:** **Near-zero — actively avoids invites, sign-ins, account linking.** Design social as strictly optional or lose her. **Top uninstall triggers:** Any login/permission wall; casino/gambling ad content; UI that's cluttered or too fast; surprise data requests.

## (c) PANEL SYNTHESIS

**Cross-archetype winners (by mean, but read the variance).** Puzzle tops the table (3.7) but is **bimodal**: it's a 5 for exactly the two least ad-monetizable players (Linda = banners-only/anti-login; Sarah = ad-averse, ~$5) and a lukewarm 3 for everyone else. Puzzle is also the most saturated red ocean (Block Blast, Royal Match) and its lovers punish ad density hardest `[EB: Block Blast "too many ads" complaint]`. **High mean, low margin for a solo IAA dev.**

**The real sweet spot for a lean, honest, IAA-first solo build is Idle/progression-lite (3.2 mean, and the flattest distribution — no 1s, only Linda at 2).** It uniquely fits every constraint: (1) **rewarded-ad-native** (double offline earnings, speed-ups) — the format players *prefer* and that drives 3.5x retention / 4.5x IAP lift `[EB: MAF/Netmarvel]`; (2) **offline + tiny build** satisfies Bilal (data/size) and Marcus (offline); (3) **no social cold-start** — it's fun for a single user, so a solo dev with a tiny launch audience isn't blocked by an empty friend graph (the fatal risk of Priya's social-hybrid genres); (4) **Marcus's reflexive remove-ads IAP** gives clean hybrid upside without building a whale economy.

**Second option: Horde-survival/survivor-like** — highest *ad-ARPU ceiling* (revive/2x-gold rewarded loops are genre-standard and players opt in enthusiastically `[EB: MiniReview]`) and best organic UA via TikTok (Hamza's discovery path). But it **polarizes** (women/older reject it, 1–2s from Sarah/Priya/Linda) and carries a heavier build that strains Bilal's device. Great if the founder accepts a narrow young-male, ad-only audience.

**Combos that alienate whom.** **Social-casual hybrid & party/board** post strong means (3.5) but are a **trap for a solo dev**: their scores are carried by Priya (5) and depend entirely on live-ops + a populated social graph + event cadence that one person cannot sustain, and they crater with Marcus (2, anti-social/online) and Linda (2, anti-login). **Aggressive interstitials** alienate the entire monetizable middle — Sarah (uninstall-class), Marcus (2nd ad = gone), and even tolerant Bilal on metered data — and the Deloitte/AdMob data says one disruptive placement costs 6–7% churn, tripling on reward screens `[EB: Deloitte/AdMob]`. **Forced login** alienates Linda, Bilal, and Marcus and statistically bleeds 23%+ of installs `[EB: Corbado]`. **Pay-to-win** alienates the two free-riders whose value is impressions and virality (Bilal, Hamza) — the Ludo Star "rigged/gems-to-win" backlash is the cautionary tale `[EB: MouthShut]`.

**First-session design implications (evidence-locked).**
- **Time-to-fun < 30 seconds** to first meaningful action; a *complete satisfying loop within 3–5 min* since median sessions run 5–6 min `[EB: GameAnalytics]`. Target top-quartile D1 (25–33%).
- **Tutorial:** minimal, teach-by-doing, skippable; long forced tutorials spike FTUE drop-off `[EB: GameAnalytics]`. (Marcus is the lone exception who'll accept depth.)
- **Build:** ≤20–25MB initial install, on-demand assets after — each 10MB saved ≈ +2.5% install conversion in Bilal's market `[EB: Google Play/Tolomei]`. **Offline core** mandatory (Marcus, Bilal).
- **No forced login** — guest-first, optional cloud link later.
- **Ad cadence:** rewarded-first and generous; interstitials rare, never on reward/end-card screens, never mid-match; make remove-ads a real, complete purchase.

**"Honest ad → real game" means, per persona:** **Bilal** — the board/action in the ad is the actual game *and* it truly runs light on his data/RAM. **Sarah** — what-you-see-is-what-you-play; no "save the king" pin-pull bait, and ads don't interrupt every level. **Hamza** — the flashy power in the ad is really achievable gameplay; rewarded ads buy power, they don't wall it. **Marcus** — no fake footage, works offline, and remove-ads kills *all* forced ads. **Priya** — the social/event fantasy shown is real and her family can actually join. **Linda** — no dark-pattern tap-bait, zero gambling ad content, and no surprise sign-in.

**Who a solo IAA-first dev should design for — and consciously ignore.**
- **DESIGN FOR: Hamza** (your rewarded-ad revenue engine + free TikTok UA), **Marcus** (your hybrid dollars via remove-ads + the offline/depth quality bar), and **Bilal** (your build-constraint conscience — size, data, heat, honest RNG). These three define an idle/progression-lite or survivor-lite game that is offline-capable, rewarded-ad-monetized, and light.
- **SWING — win if cheap: Sarah.** Huge market and genuinely monetizable ($5, remove-ads), but only if you exercise ad restraint. If your genre is puzzle-adjacent, court her by being *the honest, ad-light* option; otherwise let her go.
- **CONSCIOUSLY IGNORE (as primary targets): Priya and Linda.** Priya's spend requires live-ops, an events calendar, and a social/whale economy a solo dev cannot sustain — chasing her adds scope that sinks the project. Linda is banner-only, anti-login, anti-permission, near-zero ARPU, and rejects your growth loops — high friction for little return. Serve both only if they fall out of the core design for free (e.g., a tasteful optional daily challenge), never bend the roadmap toward them.

## STRONG CLAIMS
1. **Idle/progression-lite is the highest-expected-value genre for this specific founder** — flattest cross-persona appeal, rewarded-ad-native, offline, tiny build, no social cold-start, and a built-in hybrid IAP (remove-ads) — aligning with the constraints better than higher-mean but solo-hostile social/party genres. `[EB-anchored + PU synthesis]`
2. **Rewarded-first is non-negotiable and near-costless:** players prefer it ~4:1, it lifts retention up to 3.5x, and it's the one "payment" Bilal and Hamza (the free-rider majority) gladly give `[EB: MAF/Netmarvel]`.
3. **Interstitials are the primary churn lever to avoid:** one disruptive placement ≈ +6–7% churn, tripling on reward/end screens; Marcus uninstalls at the 2nd unskippable and Sarah's loudest grievance is ad-every-level `[EB: Deloitte/AdMob; Block Blast reviews]`.
4. **Ship ≤20–25MB and offline or lose the emerging-market install** — size/data is Bilal's hard gate and −10MB ≈ +2.5% installs `[EB: Google Play/Tolomei]`.
5. **No forced login** — guest-first play; account walls cost 23%+ of users and 30–60% of first-run retention, and directly repel Linda/Marcus/Bilal `[EB: Corbado]`.
6. **Honesty is a monetization asset, not a tax:** the ad-honest games (Royal Match's real mini-games) are rewarded while bait (Gardenscapes/Hero Wars) is resented — matching the founder's honest-creative brief to what the market already punishes/rewards `[EB: MAF/HowToGeek]`.
7. **Party/board and social-casual hybrid are a solo-founder trap:** high mean scores are an illusion propped by one persona (Priya) and a live-ops/social-graph burden one dev can't sustain `[PU synthesis on EB genre data]`.
8. **Sarah is the profit-swing persona:** the market's most monetizable casual archetype, winnable purely through ad restraint you were already going to exercise `[EB: casual-gamer ad-sensitivity]`.

## WEAK SPOTS
1. **Individual 1–5 scores are simulated judgments,** not fielded ratings; treat the *rank order and variance* as more reliable than any single cell.
2. **Priya's and Monopoly GO's social-spend-pressure claims are the least-cited** (search returned thematic, not primary, sources) — `[PU]`, verify with real r/MonopolyGOgame threads before trusting her spend numbers.
3. **Survivor.io-specific review data was thin;** Hamza's monetization tolerance is generalized from the survivor-like *genre* (Demon Survival, Pizza Hero) `[EB: MiniReview]`, not the exact title — his forced-ad tolerance may be over-stated.
4. **Bilal's zero-spend is assumed;** Easypaisa penetration means some Pakistani riders do micro-spend — his IAP floor may be marginally understated `[PU]`.
5. **Personas are archetypes, not distributions** — real segments have variance (some 55-year-olds love match-3 action; some teens hate ads). Do not read a persona as a whole cohort.
6. **Regional/platform skews compress here:** iOS-vs-Android ad and spend behavior, and MENA/EU/US/South-Asia differences, are folded into single personas; a real campaign should re-segment by geo before UA spend.
7. **All 2024–2026 benchmark figures are industry-aggregate;** your genre/geo will deviate, and several stats trace to vendor blogs (MAF, Netmarvel, Tenjin) with a promotional interest in rewarded/hybrid — directionally sound, precise magnitudes uncertain.
